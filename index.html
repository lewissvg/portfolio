02 - About

A decade of building things people use.

I'm a frontend and product engineer who's spent the last ten-plus years shipping websites, web apps and games for teams big and small.

I'm happiest somewhere between design and engineering - taking a fuzzy idea, prototyping it quickly, and grinding the details until it feels good in the hand.

My day job is the front of the stack - TypeScript, React, Pixi.js - but I write C# and Node when proofs of concept call for it, and ship native mobile when products do.

Approach
Prototype, then polish. I'd rather have a rough thing on screen by Friday than a perfect spec by month-end.
Strengths
Animation, interaction, edge cases. The unglamorous work that makes a product feel finished.
Working with
Founders & product teams who want a single owner across design polish, frontend, and the occasional native or backend stretch.
Available
Yes - for short sprints, longer engagements, or technical co-founder conversations.
